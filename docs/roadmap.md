# Roadmap

1. **Shell**: the dial, Carry (with scenes), per-mode memory, the fob type (Archivo) and chrome, motion tokens (springs, settle curve). Built and checked in the simulator first.
2. **Cards**: QR cards and the asset pipeline (photos, QR codes) into spare flash.
3. **Phone services**: notifications (ANCS), media (AMS) and time (CTS) from the iPhone with no app; pairing and settings screens, including the buried "forget this phone".
4. **The pet and the studio**: the pixel pet scene and the pack studio web page.
5. **Sleep and power**: ambient state with the processor asleep, measured on a fitted battery.

Everything is designed in the book before it is built.
